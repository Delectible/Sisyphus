Sisyphus is a focus timer (pomodoro) with a myth in it.

Choose how long to focus, how long to rest and how many rounds. While you focus, you see through Sisyphus's eyes: your hands on the stone, the slope rising under a heavy sky. When the ascent ends, the stone rolls back down and you rest in a calm green valley. After the last climb you reach the summit, with the numbers of your session and a short poem.

• Ascend and rest with a big, clear timer. Tap it to pause.
• Skip a phase or leave the mountain; your focus still counts.
• Presets: Classic 25/5, Deep 50/10, Brief 15/3, or your own.
• The ledger: mountains climbed, hours focused, streaks and your week.
• A notification when a phase ends, even with the app closed.
• Day and night: follows your phone's light or dark mode.
• Light on the battery and a small download.
• A few secrets. Try the Motivate button.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
