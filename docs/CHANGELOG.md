# Change log

Requests by number, newest last.

1. **0.1.0** First version: ascent / rest / summit scenes, timer with
   pause (tap) / skip / exit, presets, stats ledger with week chart and
   streaks, summit poems, alerts, light and dark, 60/30 fps setting, still
   scenes, boulder app icon. Easter eggs: Motivate, slipping boulder, owl,
   Breathe, lightning, Camus.
2. **0.1.1** Motivate spam keeps the ground moving smoothly: settled shoves
   fold into a running total instead of being dropped (the stones used to
   jump back after the 6th shove). Slip recovery also ends seamlessly.
3. **0.1.1** Pause eases the climb to a stop (steps and ground slow over
   ~0.7 s, then a breathing sway) and eases back in on resume. Valley:
   mountain ridges, outcrops, scree, scrub, pines, snow streaks, a temple
   on a ledge, a stream with moving light, an olive tree, a dry-stone wall,
   bushes, grazing sheep, butterflies; the still parts are cached as a
   picture so the extra detail costs nothing per frame. Home medallion:
   the boulder rests on the slope.
4. **0.2.0** Climb scene: layered ranges, a glow behind the ridge that
   grows near the top, ravens, two-tone storm clouds, crags, the worn path,
   a dead tree and a cairn on the ridge, mist, big rocks by the path,
   thistles, amphora shards, bones, rain at night and dust by day.
   Features: live Android countdown notification (each phase end posts the
   next phase's countdown); long rest (every N climbs, campfire in the
   valley); laurels (14, three secret) unlocking boulder stones (sandstone,
   marble, mossy, obsidian, bronze, gold); session labels with stats per
   label; shareable summit card; ambient sound generated in code (wind,
   stream and birds or crickets, summit breeze); keep screen on. Extras:
   metres climbed, laurel banner, laurels on the summit card, sound toggle
   on the timer.
5. **0.2.1** Sound: the valley stream, birds and crickets are gone; one
   soft wind plays through the whole session (warm brown noise trimmed
   below ~80 Hz for phone speakers, slow swells of a few dB, a minute-long
   seamless loop). Motivate: dropped "Zeus is watching" and "The gods
   tremble" (now "Keep rolling", "Almost there").
