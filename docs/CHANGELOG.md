# Change log

Requests by number, newest last.

1. **0.1.0** First version: ascent / rest / summit scenes, timer with
   pause (tap) / skip / exit, presets, stats ledger with week chart and
   streaks, summit poems, alerts, light and dark, 60/30 fps setting, still
   scenes, boulder app icon. Easter eggs: Motivate, slipping boulder, owl,
   Breathe, lightning, Camus.
2. **0.1.1** Motivate spam keeps the ground moving smoothly: settled shoves
   fold into a running total instead of being dropped (the stones used to
   jump back after the 6th shove). Slip recovery also ends seamlessly.
3. **0.1.1** Pause eases the climb to a stop (steps and ground slow over
   ~0.7 s, then a breathing sway) and eases back in on resume. Valley:
   mountain ridges, outcrops, scree, scrub, pines, snow streaks, a temple
   on a ledge, a stream with moving light, an olive tree, a dry-stone wall,
   bushes, grazing sheep, butterflies; the still parts are cached as a
   picture so the extra detail costs nothing per frame. Home medallion:
   the boulder rests on the slope.
